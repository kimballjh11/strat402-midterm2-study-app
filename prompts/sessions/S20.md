# Brief: S20 Game Theory II (8 objectives, S20-01 to S20-08)

Sources: S20_S20_Slides_Game_Theory_II (70 pages; pages 1 to 7 are announcements and the midcourse evaluation, the Beautiful Mind simultaneous game recap follows), S20_Game_Theory_Exercise (Part 1 is a one shot Facebook versus Twitter misinformation game in normal form, so it also feeds S19; Parts 2 and 3 are the repeated and sequential versions), S20_Textbook_Study_Notes_Ch_10_Repeated_Multistage (secondary, short), reading quiz.

Must teach:
- Repeated games. Finite repeated: backward induction from the last period, the last period is a one shot game, so cooperation unravels when the one shot equilibrium is unique (slide 29 is titled "Underwear Bomber Rule" and has no extractable text, so view that page image and use the professor's explanation of the finite case). Infinitely repeated: trigger (grim trigger) strategies, cooperate until a rival cheats then punish forever. Derive the condition: cooperate if the present value of cooperating forever is at least the payoff from cheating once then the punishment path. With discount factor delta (delta = 1/(1+i) for interest rate i): cooperating forever is pi_c/(1-delta), cheating gives pi_d + delta pi_n/(1-delta), so cooperation holds when delta >= (pi_d - pi_c)/(pi_d - pi_n). Not on the sheet, derive and memorize. Use the in-class exercise numbers.
- Collusion: explicit versus tacit, examples on the slides, and the factors that influence the likelihood of collusion (number of firms, differentiation, detection speed, interest rate or patience, demand stability, punishment severity, market concentration, whatever the slides and reading list, use their list exactly).
- Sequential games: extensive form (decision tree), players, strategies, payoffs, sequence. Backward induction, subgame perfect equilibrium and how it differs from Nash equilibrium (non credible threats). Slides: Obama and Putin, Trump tariffs and Xi trade game, social media and misinformation.
- Business applications of sequential games: market entry and deterrence, innovation, bargaining.
Math refreshers: present value of a perpetuity and geometric series sum 1/(1-delta), discount factor from interest rate, comparing payoff streams, tree reading.
Calculation objectives: S20-01 and S20-03 (trigger threshold and present values), S20-06 (backward induction and subgame perfect equilibrium), S20-02 (finite unraveling by logic), S20-05 (reading payoffs off an extensive form).
Graphs: tree component (editable tree with backward induction), repeated component (grim trigger with delta slider and threshold readout).
Generators: delta threshold from random payoffs, present value of a perpetual payoff stream, whether collusion is sustainable at interest rate i, backward induction on a 2 to 3 stage random tree, entry deterrence payoffs.
