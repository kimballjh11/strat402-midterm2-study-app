===== PAGE 45 =====
In-Class Exercise – Market Structure
Suppose that the cost function for your firm can be represented by the following 
function:
𝐶𝑄= 125 + 4𝑄2
Determine the profit maximizing level of output and price, and expected level of 
profits given the following scenarios. Discuss the strategic implications of each 
situation.
1. You are a price taker (i.e., perfectly competitive firm) and other firms in your 
industry charge $40/unit.
2. You are monopolist and the inverse demand function for your product is P = 100 
– Q.
3. You are a monopolistically competitive firm and the inverse demand function for 
your brand is  P = 100 – Q. 

===== PAGE 46 =====
Perfect Competition
You are a price taker (i.e. perfectly competitive firm) and 
other firms in your industry charge $40/unit.
MR = P = $40 and MC = 8Q
Optimal Output: MR = MC,   40 = 8Q,   Q = 5 
Cost of Production: C(5) = 125 + 4(5)2 = 125 + 100 = 225
Revenue: R = PQ = 40 * 5 = 200
Profits = R – C = 200 – 225 = -25
Firm will continue to operate in the short run, but exit in 
the long-run

===== PAGE 47 =====
Monopoly
You are monopolist and the inverse demand function for your 
product is P = 100 – Q.
MR = 100 – 2Q and MC = 8Q
Optimal Output: MR = MC,   100 – 2Q = 8Q,   𝑄𝑀 = 10 
Optimal Price: 𝑃𝑀= 100 −𝑄= 100 −10 = 90 
Cost of Production: C(10) = 125 + 4(10)2 = 125 + 400 = 525
Revenue: R = PQ = 90 * 10 = 900
Profits = R – C = 900 – 525 = 375
Monopolist will not face entry (unless patent or other entry barriers 
are eliminated)

===== PAGE 48 =====
Monopolistic Competition
You are a monopolistically competitive firm and the inverse demand function for your brand is  P 
= 100 – Q. 
MR = 100 – 2Q and MC = 8Q
Optimal Output: MR = MC,   100 – 2Q = 8Q,   𝑄𝑀 = 10 
Optimal Price: 𝑃𝑀= 100 −𝑄= 100 −10 = 90 
Cost of Production: C(10) = 125 + 4(10)2 = 125 + 400 = 525
Revenue: R = PQ = 90 * 10 = 900
Profits = R – C = 900 – 525 = 375
Monopolistically competitive firms should expect other firms to imitate, so profits will decline with 
entry.

Heartless Monopolists or Innovative Leaders?

2. Profits give a firm room to look past survival.
