=== PAGE 41 ===
Pricing Strategies to Limit Entry/Competition

Pricing Strategy    Definition
Limit Pricing       A strategy where an incumbent maintains a price below the
                    monopoly level in order to prevent entry
Predatory Pricing   A strategy where a firm temporarily prices below marginal cost
                    to drive competitors out of the market
Penetration         A strategy where a firm charges a low price initially to
Pricing             penetrate a market and gain critical mass of customers; useful
                    when strong network effects are present
