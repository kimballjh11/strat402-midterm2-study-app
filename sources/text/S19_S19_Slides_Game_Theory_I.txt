What should Visa do?
Visa should choose to lower their fees. Why?
▪No matter what Mastercard decides to do, Visa’s best option is to
 lower its fees.
▪Visa has a dominant strategy to lower its fees

What is a dominant strategy?

A strategy that results in the highest payoff to a player regardless of the
opponent’s actions.

=== PAGE 32 ===
Is there a Nash equilibrium?
Yes, the Nash equilibrium is for both players to lower their
fees.

What is a Nash equilibrium?

A condition describing a set of strategies in which no player can
improve his or her payoff by unilaterally changing his or her strategy,
given the other players’ strategies.

Could they collude?
Indeed, they would both be better off, it they could agree
beforehand to maintain their fees. Given their payoffs, why
wouldn’t that work?

Agreeing to maintain the status quo wouldn’t work because
both players would have an incentive to defect from the
agreement.

=== PAGE 44 ===
Mixed (Randomized) Strategy
A mixed or randomized strategy is a strategy whereby a player
randomizes over two or more available actions in order to
keep rivals from being able to predict his or her action.

Examples of mixed strategies in games, sports, etc.
  ▪Rock, Paper, Scissors
  ▪Penalty kicks
  ▪Football
