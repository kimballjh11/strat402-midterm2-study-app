=== PAGE 5 ===
Production Function
A production function defines the relationship between inputs and
outputs

                                    Q = f(K,L)

where K is the units of capital and L is the units of labor.

Types of production functions
  1. Linear
  2. Cobb-Douglas
  3. Leontief (Fixed-Proportions)

dollar spent (buck) is equal for all inputs.
