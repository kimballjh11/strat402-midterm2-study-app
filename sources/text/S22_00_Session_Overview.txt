 Chapter 9: Standard & Differentiated Bertrand Oligopolies 

 This session has two readings: the rest of Chapter 9 in the textbook and a short note on rivalry in oligopoly by BYU Marriott professor David Bryce. Read or skim both. Then answer the Session 22: Reading Quiz questions. 

 1. Textbook 

 Pick up Chapter 9: Basic Oligopoly Models where Session 21 left off, with the last of the four oligopoly settings: Bertrand competition in price. 

 Textbook 
 Baye & Prince, Managerial Economics and Business Strategy 

 Chapter 
 9 — Basic Oligopoly Models 

 Pages 
 292–299 and 305 

 Sections 
 Profit Maximization in Four Oligopoly Settings: Bertrand Oligopoly; Comparing Oligopoly Models; Contestable Markets; Answering the Headline; Summary; Appendix: Differentiated-Product Bertrand Oligopoly 

 2. Rivalry in Oligopoly 

 Read or skim Rivalry in Oligopoly . It puts all four oligopoly models side by side and spends the most time on the one you are most likely to meet in practice: differentiated Bertrand. It ends with three questions for working out which model fits a real market. 

 Format 
 PDF, 10 pages — opens in your browser 

 Author 
 David J. Bryce, BYU Marriott School of Business 

 Sections 
 Types of Competition in Oligopoly: Cournot, Bertrand, Stackelberg, Differentiated Bertrand; Determining Market Structure and Competition 

 Learning Objectives for Session 22: Oligopoly II 

 Know and understand the following economic concepts: standard Bertrand oligopoly, differentiated Bertrand oligopoly 

 Be able to identify the conditions under which a firm operates in a standard or differentiated Bertrand oligopoly 

 Be able to determine optimal decisions and likely competitor responses in a standard or differentiated Bertrand oligopoly
