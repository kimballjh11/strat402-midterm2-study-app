===== SLIDE 27 =====
Short Run vs. Long Run
In the short-run, we typically think of capital like the number of grills fixed and the number of employees as variable
But in the long-run, a manager is free to adjust the levels of all inputs
Buy more machines (e.g. grills)
Build more plants (locations)
In the long-run all costs are variable

Economies of Scale
===== SLIDE 29 =====
Economies of Scale
The 
long-run average cost curve 
defines the minimum average cost of producing alternative levels of output, allowing for optimal selection of fixed and variable factors of production
Economies of scale 
exist when the long-run average costs decline as output is increased.
Diseconomies of scale 
exist when long-run average costs rise as output is increased
