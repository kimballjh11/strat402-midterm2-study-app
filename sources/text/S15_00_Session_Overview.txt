 In-Class Exercise 

 J Dawgs 

 J Dawgs sells hot dogs out of a small stand near campus with two grills, and the only choice left is how many workers to put behind the counter. In this exercise you'll calculate the marginal and average product of labor, find where adding workers starts to bring diminishing returns, and use the value of the marginal product to decide how many people to hire. Download the workbook below and work through the questions with the people around you.
