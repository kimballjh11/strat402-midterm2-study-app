=== PAGE 20 ===
Production Function
A production function defines the relationship between
inputs and outputs (i.e., it tells us the rate at which a
company generates output given a certain level of
input)

                        Q = f(K,L)

where K is the units of capital and L is the units of labor

=== PAGE 21 ===
Conceptual Definitions
Total product (Q) – the maximum level of output that can be
produced with a given amount of inputs

Average product (Q/L) – the output produced per unit of
input

Marginal product (ΔQ/ ΔL or dQ/dL) – the change in total
output attributable to the last unit of an input

=== PAGE 22 ===
The Role of a Strategist
In the short run…
  1. Ensure that the firm operates at it maximum level of
     efficiency
  2. Ensure that the firm uses the correct level of input(s)

In the long run…
  3. Ensure that the firm uses the optimal mix of inputs

=== PAGE 25 ===
Marginal Returns
Increasing marginal returns (IMR) – range of input
usage over which marginal product increases

Diminishing marginal returns (DMR) – range of input
usage over which marginal product declines

Negative marginal returns (NMR) – range of input
usage over which marginal product is negative
